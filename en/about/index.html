What OPTES is

  • OPTES Code: an app for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), with Linux builds (.deb and AppImage), for coding and working on projects and files.
  • OPTES on the web: a private workspace per account that you open from the browser, with the same account, plan and credit.
  • The developers platform: the model catalog and API keys.
  • The site comes in 16 languages, and plans are paid in Iraqi dinars (IQD) through local payment in Iraq, and in US dollars with cryptocurrency everywhere else.

The owner stays in control

Our policy: changes to the site and its sections are reviewed before publication and require the owner's approval or a previously approved operating policy. Automation operates within that scope; oversight and responsibility remain with the owner.

What we believe

That technology should work in people's own language, whatever it is, with its dialects as they are spoken, and that paying should work where you live, without a foreign card. And that we should say plainly what we know and what we do not: we do not promise a result we have not tested, and we fix a mistake when we learn of it.
