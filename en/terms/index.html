Who we are and what the service is

The service covers the OPTES Code app, the web edition, the phone apps, the developers platform and this site. It is provided by the OPTES project, based in Baghdad, Iraq. Contact: support@optes3.com.

Some models and features are for adults only and are marked 18+ in the apps and on the plans page (today: OPTES 1.2.1 Flash, OPTES 1.2 Series, OPTES 1.2 Super and the MORPHEUS models). You may use them only if you are 18 or older; they open only after you confirm your age, and a parent's or guardian's consent does not replace that confirmation. Confirming an age that is not yours breaks these terms. An account is for one person: do not share a paid account or sell access to it.

Plans and payment

Plans are monthly and can be paid in two ways: in Iraqi dinars through the Wayl gateway, or in cryptocurrency through the OxaPay gateway. In Iraq the checkout opens on Wayl; outside Iraq it opens on cryptocurrency. When you pay in dinars, the amount shown is the amount you pay. Each plan has a weekly limit that renews every seven days and a monthly allowance, as shown on the plans page.

Paying with cryptocurrency. The amount is the plan's published US-dollar price, based on 1,640 IQD per USD and rounded down to a price ending in .49 or .99 (the same price inside and outside Iraq), and you pay it in USDT, BTC, ETH or another coin and network the checkout page offers. Each invoice is valid for two hours: send the exact amount, on the network shown, to the address shown, before it expires. Funds sent to that address after the invoice has expired may be lost, and we may not be able to recover them. Your wallet's or exchange's fees are yours; the gateway's fee is ours. The plan starts once the network confirms the payment and the full amount has arrived (we accept a shortfall of up to 0.50 USD for network rounding). Blockchain payments cannot be reversed, so check the coin, network, address and amount before you send.

When a crypto payment needs review. If you send less or more than asked, in a coin or network the invoice did not offer, or after the invoice ended, nothing is activated automatically: the payment goes to a manual review, and you can write to support@optes3.com with the invoice reference if we have not contacted you. After the review we either activate what you paid for or send the amount back to a wallet address you give us; if you paid more than asked, we activate the plan and send back the difference the same way.

Plans do not renew automatically and nothing is charged without you acting: seven days before your plan ends we send you a payment link, you have three days' grace after the end, and then the plan ends and you return to the free plan if you have not paid.

Payments are not refundable, except when you were charged twice by mistake, paid and your plan was not activated, or in the crypto review cases above; in the first two cases write to us within 14 days and we refund the amount or activate the plan. A cryptocurrency payment is refunded to a wallet address you give us. Prices may change; we announce a change at least seven days ahead, and it never affects a period you have already paid for.
