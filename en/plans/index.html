How credit works

  1. Your monthly credit is split into weeks: weekly credit = monthly × 7 ÷ 30.
  2. A subscriber's week starts on the day they subscribed. The free plan's week renews every Saturday.
  3. If you finish your week early, about a seventh of the week opens for you every day, so you do not wait for the week to end.
  4. What you do not spend in a quiet week carries over to the next one. The monthly credit stays the ceiling.
  5. Your last message still goes through even with only 300 credits left.
  6. The daily pictures and clips come out of the same credit: a fast picture about 105 credits, a normal picture about 525, a clip about 6,000.
  7. A voice note (speech to text) uses 60 credits a minute.

Questions

Questions about the plans

How do I pay?

Outside Iraq: in US dollars, in cryptocurrency (USDT, BTC, ETH and more) through OxaPay, at the dollar price shown on this page. In Iraq: in Iraqi dinars (IQD) through local payment, from «My account», fees included. The rules for both are in the Terms of Use.

What is the weekly credit?

Your monthly credit is split into weeks: weekly credit = monthly × 7 ÷ 30. A subscriber's week starts on the day they subscribed. The free plan's week renews every Saturday. If you finish your week early, about a seventh of the week opens for you every day, so you do not wait for the week to end. What you do not spend in a quiet week carries over to the next one. The monthly credit stays the ceiling. Your last message still goes through even with only 300 credits left.

Are the daily pictures and clips free?

No. The daily allowance says how many pictures and clips you can make a day, but each one is taken from your plan's credit: a fast picture about 105 credits, a normal picture about 525, a clip about 6,000. The allowance comes back at midnight UTC+3.

How many messages can I send?

A message uses 457 credits on average, and half of all messages use less than 43 (measured over 7 days up to 2026-11-05). So the number changes a lot with how long the work is.

Are the free models unlimited?

The free models (OPTES Fast) are not unlimited: on the Free plan, 420 messages a week; for subscribers they use very little credit (about 1 per step).

What are the MORPHEUS (unrestricted) models?

Models that refuse less in adult creative writing and in legal and sensitive topics. From Pro 2x ($17.99) and up. They open only after you confirm you are 18 or older. They use much more credit: a step costs on average about 4 times a regular step, some up to 9 times. OPTES's usage policy and the law still apply: no illegal content, nothing involving minors, no harm to others.

Which plans have the cloud computer?

From Pro 2x ($17.99) up: Pro 2x 2 hours a day and 5 GB, Pro+ 3 hours and 10 GB, Max 6 hours and 30 GB, Max Plus 10 hours and 60 GB, Max Ultra 16 hours and 120 GB. Each has 2 GB RAM and 1.5 vCPU. It turns itself off after 2 hours with nobody connected, and your files are kept. The hours come back at midnight UTC+3.

Can I cancel?

Yes. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for.

What does «Phone only» mean on Lite?

Lite is a chat plan: it works in the phone app and in the web chat, on the same account. It does not include the desktop app (agent and editor), the cloud computer or video. You subscribe from the phone app; for the desktop app, choose Pro or higher.
