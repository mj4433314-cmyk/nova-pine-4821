Customer support teams

An AI assistant for customer support teams, for quick, clear replies

Every customer message deserves a quick, kind and accurate reply, even on the busiest days. OPTES writes saved replies in your brand's voice, suggests calm wording for difficult complaints, turns product manuals into a help centre and analyses tickets to reveal why customers keep getting in touch. Your agents review and send; the customer relationship stays with your team.

What OPTES does for you

Saved reply library

It writes replies to frequent questions in your brand's voice, in standard Arabic or in the dialect the customer writes in. You review them before the team adopts them.

An example of what you type

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help customer support teams?

OPTES helps your team reply quickly and consistently: saved replies, drafts for difficult complaints, a help centre from your documents, ticket analysis and call transcripts. Your agents review and send every reply.

Does OPTES reply to customers directly?

Today, OPTES suggests the reply and your agent sends it after review.

Does it understand messages written in dialect?

Yes. It understands what customers write in standard Arabic or dialect and replies in the language you choose. For call transcripts it converts spoken Arabic, dialects included, into text; check names and numbers.

How do we protect customer data?

Hide phone numbers, addresses and payment details when the task does not need them, and follow your company's policies. OPTES hides secrets such as keys and passwords from the agent, and what it did stays logged in front of you.
