Content and video creators

An AI assistant for content creators, from idea to every asset

Tell OPTES the idea for your next video, in any language or dialect. It drafts the hook and script, generates clips from a description or a photo, designs a thumbnail, writes music that fits the pace and reads the voice-over in natural Arabic or in your own voice, with your permission. When you film yourself, it turns your speech into text for captions.

What OPTES does for you

Write the video script

Drafts a script split into hook, beats and close, timed to the length you set and written in your channel's tone. You read it and adjust the tone and examples before filming.

An example of what you type

Write a 45-second reel script on common phone-filming mistakes, with a strong first line and a light, casual tone

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Clips from an idea or a photo

Generates short clips from a written description or an image you upload, for B-roll, transitions or backgrounds. You review each clip and ask for another take if it misses your style.

An example of what you type

Turn this cafe photo into a five-second clip with a slow push-in toward the door and soft morning light

AvailableVideo generation

Thumbnails that stand out

Proposes several thumbnail designs with short bold text and colours that hold their own in a crowded feed. You pick one and ask for changes to the background or wording.

An example of what you type

Design three thumbnails for 'I spent a week without my phone': a surprised face and large Arabic text

AvailableImage generation

Background music cut to length

Composes music in the mood and length you need, calm under an explanation or upbeat for an intro. You listen and ask for a shorter version or a different rhythm.

An example of what you type

I need one minute of background music for a cooking video: calm, building up in the last ten seconds

AvailableMusic

Voice-over in your voice or a natural Arabic voice

Reads your script in natural Arabic speech, or in your own voice once you give permission to clone it, never anyone else's. You listen and correct how names are pronounced.

An example of what you type

Read this script in my saved voice, energetic at the start and calmer during the explanation

AvailableVoice

Your speech as caption text

Upload a recording and it turns your speech, dialect included, into text split into short lines for captions or the post description. You check names and terms before using it.

An example of what you type

Transcribe my speech in this video into short lines, and fix spelling only without changing the dialect

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help content and video creators?

It handles the work that comes before you post: scripts, generated clips, images and thumbnails, music, voice-over and transcripts of your own recordings. You make the final call at every step.

Can it speak in my voice?

Yes. Once you explicitly give permission, it can clone your own voice, and only yours, to read your scripts and voice-overs. Cloning anyone else's voice or imitating public figures is not allowed.

Does it understand my dialect when I type or speak?

Yes. Write in Modern Standard Arabic or your own dialect and it replies the same way, and speech to text handles Arabic dialects. Check names and specialist terms before the text goes on screen.
