Writers and novelists

AI assistant for writers: it edits and researches, you hold the pen

OPTES reads your draft the way a seasoned editor would: it flags sagging pace, stiff dialogue and contradictions between chapters, and suggests changes that keep your voice instead of replacing it. It researches the period and place behind your story with sources, reads your handwritten notebooks and proposes images for your cover. The story is yours; it serves it.

What OPTES does for you

Edit a chapter without losing your voice

Reads the chapter and suggests changes to pace, dialogue and repetition, explaining each one without rewriting your style. You accept what convinces you and reject the rest.

An example of what you type

Review this chapter: the dialogue between Salma and her father feels forced. Suggest edits that keep my language as it is

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Background research for the story

Gathers details on place, period, customs and dress, summarised with sources, so you can verify them yourself before you build a scene on them.

An example of what you type

I need details on daily life in Baghdad's Shorja market in the 1950s: goods, sounds and trades, with sources

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Characters and timeline

Pulls characters, ages and event dates from the manuscript and flags contradictions, like a character whose age shifts between chapters. You decide how to fix them.

An example of what you type

Build a table of the characters and their ages in each chapter, and flag any contradiction in the dates

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Handwritten notebooks to text

Photograph your notebook pages and it reads your Arabic handwriting into editable text. You check the text against the original page before it joins the draft.

An example of what you type

These are photos of three pages from my notebook. Turn them into text exactly as written, with no corrections

AvailableReading images and documents

Cover concepts

Generates cover concepts from your novel's description and mood, to show your designer or publisher or to use directly. You choose and ask for changes to colour and composition.

An example of what you type

Suggest four cover images for a novel about an old house on the Tigris: faded colours, a feeling of nostalgia, no people

AvailableImage generation

Dictate ideas on the move

Record a scene idea while walking, in Arabic or your dialect, and it turns it into tidy written notes. You review them and move what you want into the draft.

An example of what you type

Transcribe this recording as tidy notes and keep the suggested dialogue on separate lines from the description

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help writers and novelists?

It works as an editor and research assistant: it reviews your drafts and explains every suggested change, researches your story's background with sources, reads handwritten or recorded notes and proposes cover images.

Will it change my writing style?

Not unless you ask it to. You can ask for suggestions only, each with a reason, so the final sentence is always your decision and your voice stays the one on the page.

Can I rely on the historical details it finds?

It gives summaries with their sources, but checking them is your job as the author, especially dates, names and details that knowledgeable readers will notice. Go back to the source before building a scene on it.
