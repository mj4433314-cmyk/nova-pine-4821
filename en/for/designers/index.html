Designers

An AI assistant for designers that understands Arabic type and RTL

OPTES works alongside you at every stage around the design file. It generates moodboards and visual directions from a brief, reads a screenshot of your layout and critiques hierarchy, contrast and Arabic typography, and writes interface copy in Arabic and English. Then it turns the design into a working page checked at every screen size, and handles repetitive file chores.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help designers?

It generates moodboards, critiques layout and typography, writes interface copy in two languages, turns your design into a working page and handles repetitive file chores. The final visual call is always yours.

Does it replace my design tool?

No, it works around your design file: ideas, critique, copy and code. Your layouts, design systems and final decisions stay in your hands and in your tools.

Does it understand Arabic typography and right-to-left layouts?

Yes. OPTES handles Arabic properly: it spots letter-join and line-spacing issues in screenshots, builds pages in the correct direction, and checks them at each screen size to show what breaks.

Can I show a client a working prototype?

Yes. It builds clickable HTML pages from your layouts and, once you have seen the cost and approved, publishes them as a website you can share with the client.
