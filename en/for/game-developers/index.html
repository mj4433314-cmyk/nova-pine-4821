Game developers

An AI assistant for game developers, from idea to a playable build

OPTES shortens the distance between an idea and testing it. It builds a prototype with a world, characters, movement and sound so you can feel the gameplay before committing team time, and it writes and fixes systems code inside your project. It also generates concept art and temp music, and drafts quest text and dialogue in Arabic and English.

What OPTES does for you

Prototype a mechanic

Describe the mechanic and OPTES builds a playable prototype with the world, character movement and sound, so you can test the feel before the art team starts.

An example of what you type

Make a small top-down prototype: a delivery scooter in narrow alleys, a timer, and a penalty for hitting market stalls.

AvailableGame creation

Write and fix gameplay code

It reads your project, writes the system in C++ or your engine's scripting language, and tests it. Every change arrives as a diff you accept or roll back.

An example of what you type

Stamina drains twice when the player sprints and jumps at the same time. Find the duplicate call and fix it.

AvailableCoding and full projects

Concept art for characters and places

Generate concept sheets for characters, props and locations from your written brief, then iterate on the directions your art lead picks.

An example of what you type

Concept art for an old seaside café on an Arab city corniche at sunset: three directions, warm light, readable from a high camera.

AvailableImage generation

Temp music for levels

OPTES writes music for a level or main menu so the team can playtest with the right mood until the final score arrives from your composer.

An example of what you type

A 90-second loop for a night-market level: oud and light percussion, tense but not scary.

AvailableMusic

Dialogue and quest text in two languages

It drafts barks, quest descriptions and item text in Arabic and English, keeps each character's voice consistent, and flags lines too long for your UI boxes.

An example of what you type

Write lines for the fish seller on the corniche: five greetings and three refusals, in a light Basrawi dialect with an English version.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help game developers?

It builds playable prototypes, writes and fixes systems code in your project, generates concept art and temp music, and drafts dialogue in two languages. Design and quality decisions stay with your team.

Does it replace my game engine?

No, it works alongside it. It writes and fixes code in your project repository, while its game-creation capability builds worlds, characters, movement and sound for prototypes that test ideas or for complete small games.

Is there an example of what the OPTES team built?

Yes, the NightDistrict showcase on this site: a corniche of an Arab city by the sea, built in Unreal Engine 5 by the OPTES team, presented as a film and a playable version where you walk and drive.

Can it write natural Arabic for my game?

Yes. OPTES handles Arabic properly: it writes in Modern Standard Arabic or the dialect that fits your characters, translates between Arabic and English with UI length in mind, and leaves code in its own language.
