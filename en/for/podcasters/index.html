Podcasters and voice-over artists

An AI assistant for podcasters, from episode prep to transcript

OPTES preps the episode with you before you record: it researches your guest and topic with sources and lines up questions and talking points. After the recording it transcribes the conversation, dialects included, and writes the description, an outline of topics and quotes to share. It composes your theme music and reads ads and intros in natural Arabic or in your own voice, with permission.

What OPTES does for you

Guest and episode prep

Researches your guest's background, work and past interviews, summarises them with sources, then proposes talking points and questions. You verify the facts and choose the questions that fit your style.

An example of what you type

My next guest is an agricultural engineer working on hydroponics projects in Basra. Summarise what's been published about this work there, with sources, and suggest ten questions

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Episode transcript

Upload the audio and it turns the conversation into written text, even when guests speak different dialects. You check names and terms before publishing the transcript.

An example of what you type

Transcribe the whole episode and leave the English phrases exactly as they were said

AvailableSpeech to text

Show notes and quotes

From the transcript it writes a short description, an outline of topics, alternative titles and short quotes for social posts. You adjust the wording and match every quote against the recording.

An example of what you type

From this transcript, write an 80-word episode description, five alternative titles and three short quotes to share

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Theme music and stings

Composes a short opening theme and transitions between segments in the mood you describe, so your show keeps a consistent sound from episode to episode. You listen to the versions and choose.

An example of what you type

I need a 15-second opening theme for a podcast about entrepreneurship: a confident beat with a modern take on the oud

AvailableMusic

Ads and intros read aloud

Reads ad copy or the episode intro in natural Arabic speech, or in your own cloned voice with your permission, never anyone else's. You listen and approve the take you want.

An example of what you type

Read the episode intro in my saved voice, warm in tone, slowing down on the guest's name

AvailableVoice

Episode cover art

Generates a square cover for each episode with its title, in keeping with your show's look. You pick the design and ask for changes to colours or text.

An example of what you type

Design the cover for episode 12, 'From zero to the first client', in the show's colours: dark blue and orange

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help podcasters and voice-over artists?

It preps episodes with research and questions, transcribes recordings, writes descriptions, titles and quotes, composes theme music, and reads scripts in natural Arabic or in your own voice with your permission.

Can it clone my guest's voice or a famous presenter's?

No. Voice cloning is for your own voice only, and only after your explicit permission. It is not used to imitate guests, presenters or anyone else, even as a joke or a promo.

Can it handle a conversation in more than one dialect?

Yes. Speech to text handles different Arabic dialects within the same episode. Always check names of people and places and specialist terms before you publish the transcript or quote a line from it.

Do I need technical skills to use it?

No. You type your request in Arabic as you would to a colleague, in Modern Standard Arabic or your dialect, and upload your audio. The OPTES CODE app runs on Windows 10 and 11 with a right-to-left interface.
