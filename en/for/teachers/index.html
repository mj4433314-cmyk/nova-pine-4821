Teachers

An AI assistant for teachers that lightens lesson prep and paperwork

OPTES prepares lesson plans, worksheets and quizzes with you at levels that fit your class, reads students' handwritten answers to suggest feedback, designs illustrations and teaching aids, and drafts clear, kind messages to parents. Grades and teaching decisions always stay with you; it gives you back time for the students themselves.

What OPTES does for you

Lesson plan ready to adapt

Give it the subject, grade, duration and objectives and it writes a plan with a warm-up, an activity and a closing check. You adapt it to your students and your school's curriculum.

Read student work and suggest feedback

Photograph students' handwritten answers and it reads them and suggests feedback against the marking criteria you set. You decide the final grade after reviewing.

Illustrations and teaching aids

Generates illustrations for the lesson, like the water cycle or a scene from a reading story, in a style suited to your students' age. You pick what serves the lesson and ask for changes.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help teachers?

It cuts down prep and paperwork: lesson plans, differentiated worksheets, quizzes, reading handwritten student work, teaching aids and messages to parents. Teaching decisions and grades stay with the teacher.

Does it mark tests and assign grades?

It reads answers and suggests feedback against your criteria, but the grade is your decision after review. That keeps you fair to every student and lets you catch what any automated tool might miss.

Does it follow my country's curriculum?

It writes from the details you give: grade, subject, unit and lesson objectives, and you can upload pages from the set textbook for it to build on. Always check the content against your school's approved curriculum.

What about student privacy?

Upload only what the task needs, and you can remove student names before uploading their work. Always follow your school's policy on sharing student data and grades with any tool.
