Mobile app developers

An AI assistant for mobile app developers, from screen to store page

OPTES works in your app project the way you do. It builds screens in Kotlin, Swift or Dart from a mockup image, makes layouts right-to-left ready, and traces a crash from the stack trace to the line that caused it. It also builds your app's landing page, checks it at phone size, and drafts release notes in Arabic and English.

Build a screen from a mockup

Share the design image. OPTES reads the layout, spacing and text, then writes the screen with your project's components and state pattern for you to review before merging.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help mobile app developers?

It writes and fixes code inside your app project, reads design images and crash screenshots, makes layouts right-to-left ready and drafts release and store text. You review every change before you keep it.

Which languages does it write?

Whatever your project already uses, such as Kotlin, Swift, Dart or JavaScript, for native or cross-platform apps. It follows your existing architecture instead of imposing a new one.

Does it understand right-to-left problems?

Yes. OPTES handles Arabic properly. It catches margins and icons that don't mirror, clipped labels and the direction of numbers and input fields, and fixes them in the code rather than on one screen.

Does it upload my app to the app stores?

No, store submission stays with you. OPTES prepares the code, release notes and store text. One-click publishing is for websites, such as your app's landing page, after you approve the cost.
