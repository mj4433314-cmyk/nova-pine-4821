Doctors and healthcare staff

An AI assistant for doctors and healthcare staff, for the paperwork

Admin and writing work takes more of a clinician's time than it should. OPTES summarises research with sources, drafts patient-education leaflets for the doctor to review, translates forms and transcribes meeting notes. It is an assistant for administrative and writing tasks only; it does not diagnose or prescribe, and the qualified professional always decides.

Important

OPTES is an assistant for administrative and writing work; it does not replace a doctor or any healthcare professional. It does not diagnose, prescribe or make clinical decisions. The qualified professional decides, and every output must be reviewed before use. Protect patient confidentiality, remove all identifying details before sharing anything, and follow your institution's rules and your professional obligations.

What OPTES does for you

Summarising research

OPTES searches available scientific sources and summarises studies on a question you set, with a link to each source, so you read the original and judge the evidence yourself.

An example of what you type

Summarise the most recent published reviews on health education programmes for people with diabetes in primary care, with links to the sources.

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Patient leaflet drafts

It drafts a plain-language leaflet on a topic the doctor chooses, from information the doctor provides. The doctor reviews and edits it before it is handed out.

An example of what you type

Draft a plain-language leaflet for patients on preparing for a fasting blood test. I will review it before printing.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Translating forms and instructions

It translates registration forms and administrative instructions between Arabic and English, keeping the field order. A qualified colleague reviews the translation before it is used.

An example of what you type

Translate this patient registration form from English into Arabic and keep the same order of fields and headings.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Department meeting minutes

It turns a recording of an admin meeting, made with everyone's consent, into minutes with action points and owners, without including any patient-identifying details.

An example of what you type

Transcribe this morning's department meeting and list what we agreed as tasks, with the person responsible for each unit.

AvailableSpeech to text

Digitising paper schedules

It reads photos of paper schedules and admin forms, such as duty rotas and supply requests, and turns them into an editable sheet you check before use.

An example of what you type

Turn this photo of the handwritten duty rota for this month into a spreadsheet organised by day.

AvailableReading images and documents

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help doctors and healthcare staff?

OPTES lightens admin and writing work: research summaries with sources, patient leaflet drafts, form translation and meeting transcripts. Clinical work and every decision stay with the doctor and qualified staff.

Does OPTES diagnose or suggest treatment?

No. OPTES is an assistant and does not replace a doctor. It does not diagnose, prescribe or make clinical decisions. What it writes are drafts and summaries for the professional to review and decide on.

How should I handle patient data?

Remove names, numbers and anything that identifies a patient before sharing any text, and follow your institution's rules and professional obligations. Do not rely on any tool to make your work compliant; that responsibility stays with your institution and you.

Can I rely on the research summaries?

Treat them as a starting point only. OPTES gives links to its sources so you can read the original and judge the study's quality yourself, as it can get a figure wrong or miss an important detail.
