Marketers

An AI assistant for marketers, from campaign idea to launch

OPTES writes ad copy and posts in several variants for testing, in Modern Standard Arabic or your audience's dialect. It generates campaign images and short videos from an idea or a product photo, researches your market and competitors with sources, and reads performance reports to suggest what to change. It builds your landing page and publishes it in one click once you see the cost and approve.

What OPTES does for you

Ad copy variants to test

Writes several versions of the same ad from different angles, such as price, quality or delivery speed, sized for each platform. You choose which to test and adjust the tone.

An example of what you type

Write five versions of an ad for a grill restaurant in Karrada, each from a different angle, in a light Baghdadi dialect

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Campaign images in platform sizes

Generates product and campaign images in your brand style, sized for posts, stories and covers. You check colours and details against the real product before publishing.

An example of what you type

Show this juice bottle on a summer table with ice and lemons, in a square version and a vertical one for stories

AvailableImage generation

Short video ads

Turns a product photo or campaign idea into a short video for ads and stories. You watch it and ask for a different shot or camera move before approving.

An example of what you type

Turn this watch photo into a six-second clip with the camera slowly circling it against a dark background

AvailableVideo generation

Market and competitor research

Gathers competitors' public offers and messaging and summarises them in a table with sources. You verify the information and position your brand from there.

An example of what you type

Summarise how online furniture stores in Iraq market their Eid offers, with source links and a comparison table

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Read performance reports

Upload screenshots or exported campaign reports and it reads the numbers, explains what went up or down and suggests what to try next. You confirm the figures in your ads dashboard.

An example of what you type

Here is a screenshot of two campaigns over a week. Which had the lower cost per click, and what should I try next week?

AvailableReading images and documents

Landing pages published with your approval

Builds a landing page for your offer and shows it to you at desktop and phone sizes, then publishes it in one click once you see the cost and approve.

An example of what you type

Make a landing page for our opening offer with the headline, the offer and a contact button, and show it to me before publishing

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help marketers?

It shortens the campaign cycle: copy in several variants, images and short videos, competitor research with sources, reading performance reports, and landing pages built and published after your approval. Strategy and decisions stay yours.

Can it write in my audience's dialect?

Yes. It writes in Modern Standard Arabic or the dialect you ask for, whether Iraqi, Gulf, Levantine or another. Test the variants with your real audience and review any local expression before it runs in a paid ad.

Will it publish pages or spend money on its own?

No. One-click publishing happens only after you see the cost and approve, and computer control needs your approval too. You can also set a spending cap that stops any paid work at your limit.

Do generated images show my product accurately?

They start from your product photo or description, but always check colours, shape and details before publishing, so customers never see something in the ad that differs from what they receive.
