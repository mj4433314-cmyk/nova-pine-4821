Online store owners

An AI assistant for online store owners, from listing to launch

Every new product needs a convincing description, attractive images and a tidy page, and that takes time you rarely have. OPTES writes product descriptions in Arabic and English, generates images to showcase them, and builds or improves your store website while you preview it on phone and desktop, then publishes it in one click after you approve.

What OPTES does for you

Product descriptions in bulk

Give it your product file as CSV and it writes a title, description and feature bullets for each item in your store's voice. You review everything before uploading it to your store dashboard.

An example of what you type

Here is a file with 80 perfumes. Write a short Arabic description for each one covering the notes, how long it lasts and the right occasion.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Seasonal and campaign images

OPTES generates images with backgrounds and scenes that suit your product and season, for banners, offers and occasions. You pick the ones that match your brand and show the product honestly.

An example of what you type

Generate an image of a premium date box on a Ramadan table with warm lighting, sized for the store's main banner.

AvailableImage generation

Store pages and changes

It builds or edits your store pages in code, such as the home page, product page and returns policy, then tests its work and reviews its own changes before showing you.

An example of what you type

Add a White Friday deals page to my store site with a countdown timer and a grid of discounted products.

Checking every screen size

OPTES sees your store running at desktop, tablet and phone sizes, spots cut-off buttons and stretched images, and you fix them with one click.

Publishing the new version

When the changes are ready, OPTES moves the project from your PC to your website in one click after you see the cost and approve. Checkpoints stay available to roll files back.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help online store owners?

OPTES handles the repetitive work in your store: writing product descriptions, generating showcase images, and building and editing site pages, then publishing them after you approve. Pricing, stock and promotions stay your decisions.

How do I get the descriptions into my store?

OPTES gives you the descriptions as a CSV file or formatted text that you upload to your store dashboard the way you normally do. If your store site is a project on your PC, it edits the code directly and you preview before publishing.

Can generated images replace product photos?

Generated images work well for banners, offers and seasonal scenes. For the product itself, a real photo is wiser, or at least make sure the image shows the item exactly as the customer will receive it, to keep their trust.

Could a change go live without my knowing?

No. Publishing only happens after you see the cost and approve, and in the approvals mode you choose what needs your explicit OK. If you dislike a result, you roll the files back to an earlier checkpoint.
