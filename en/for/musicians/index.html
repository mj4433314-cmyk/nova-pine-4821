Musicians

An AI assistant for musicians, from rehearsal idea to release day

OPTES helps with the work that surrounds the music. It writes musical sketches to test a mood or direction, turns your spoken notes after rehearsal into an organised list, types up handwritten lyric pages, and suggests rhymes and alternative lines. It also generates short release visuals and builds your artist page. Composition, performance and the final sound remain yours.

What OPTES does for you

Sketch an arrangement idea

Describe the mood, tempo and instruments, and OPTES writes a musical sketch to react to, as a starting point for your own arrangement or to show your band a direction.

An example of what you type

A sketch in maqam Bayati, 6/8 at 96 BPM, oud and frame drum, building slowly into the chorus.

AvailableMusic

Rehearsal voice notes

Record your ideas after rehearsal in your own words and dialect. It turns them into text, then into an organised list of ideas, changes and tasks for next session.

An example of what you type

I recorded notes after rehearsal. Type them up and sort them: what we change in the intro, what we redo in the chorus, who brings the new rhythm.

AvailableSpeech to text

Refine your lyrics

It suggests rhymes and alternative lines that respect the metre you set, from classical verse to dialect songs, and drafts credits and liner notes. Which words stay is your call.

An example of what you type

This is the second verse; line three sits heavily on the melody. Suggest three alternatives with the same rhyme, in Baghdadi dialect.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Handwritten lyric pages

Photograph your notebook pages, including Arabic handwriting. OPTES types them up, keeps your line breaks, and marks any word it couldn't read for you to confirm.

An example of what you type

Here are photos of five notebook pages. Type them up exactly with the same line breaks, and mark any word you're unsure of.

AvailableReading images and documents

Short release visuals

From your cover image or an idea, it generates a short looping video to announce a single or to sit behind the lyrics, and you choose what to use.

An example of what you type

From this cover image, make a short looping video: calm waves and moonlight moving slowly, for the single announcement.

AvailableVideo generation

Your artist page

It writes your bio in Arabic and English and builds an artist page with links to your music and photos, published only after you see the cost and approve.

An example of what you type

Write a short bio about me in two languages and build an artist page with song links, concert photos and a booking contact.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help musicians?

With the work around the music: sketches to test ideas, typed-up rehearsal notes, lyric refinement, handwritten pages transcribed, release visuals and an artist page. The creative work itself stays yours.

Does it replace my composing or playing?

No. Its musical sketches are starting points to react to, a way to test a mood or share a direction with your band. Composition, performance and the final sound are your work.

Can it write lyrics in dialect?

Yes. It writes and edits in Modern Standard Arabic or your dialect, suggests rhymes and alternative lines, and respects the metre you set. Which lines make the song is always your decision.

Can it help with a website for a new release?

Yes. It writes the bio and builds a release page or artist page, checks it at phone size, and publishes it only after you have seen the cost and approved.
