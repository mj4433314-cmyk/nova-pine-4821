Real estate agents

An AI assistant for real estate agents, from listing to follow-up

Between viewings and phone calls, there is little time left for writing. OPTES turns your property notes into a clear listing in Arabic and English, makes a short clip from the photos, builds a page for each property that you publish after approving, and tidies up your voice notes after every viewing.

What OPTES does for you

Writing the listing

Give it the size, rooms, location and features, and it writes an appealing, accurate listing in Arabic and English. You confirm every detail before it goes out.

An example of what you type

140 square metre flat in Al-Mansour, three bedrooms and a living room, third floor with a lift and parking. Write the listing in Arabic and English.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help real estate agents?

OPTES cuts the desk work around every deal: writing listings, making videos and staging images, building property pages and turning your voice notes into text. Negotiation and the client relationship stay with you.

Can I use generated images in a listing?

Use them for illustration only, such as staging concepts, and label them clearly as illustrative. Show the property's real condition with real photos, to protect buyers' trust and your reputation.

Can it write for international buyers?

Yes. It writes listings in Arabic and English and translates between them, keeping property terms and figures intact. Check units, areas and prices before publishing.

Does it understand voice notes in my dialect?

Yes. OPTES converts speech to text, including Arabic dialects, then organises it into points. Check important names and numbers, since recordings made on the street or on site can be noisy.
