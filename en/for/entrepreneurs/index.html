Entrepreneurs

An AI assistant for entrepreneurs, from first idea to launch

Every new venture starts with many questions and little time. OPTES gathers market and competitor research with sources, drafts your business plan and investor deck, then builds a landing page or working prototype you preview on desktop and phone before it goes live with your approval. You set the direction and review; it handles the detail.

What OPTES does for you

Market and competitor research

OPTES searches available sources for market size, competitors and their pricing, then gives you an organised summary with links so you can check every figure before building on it.

An example of what you type

Find the main grocery delivery apps operating in Baghdad and Basra and compare their delivery fees and payment options, with sources.

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Business plan draft

It turns scattered notes into a business plan with the familiar sections: problem, solution, market, revenue model and team. You review and adjust the numbers and assumptions yourself.

An example of what you type

Here are my notes on a central kitchen selling healthy meal subscriptions. Turn them into a ten-page business plan with clear sections.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Investor deck

It shapes your story into short slides, writes each slide and its speaker notes, and lists the hard questions investors are likely to ask so you can rehearse your answers.

An example of what you type

Write a 12-slide investor deck for a football pitch booking platform, and end with the toughest questions investors might ask.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Landing page to test demand

OPTES builds a landing page with a sign-up form, you preview it live at desktop, tablet and phone sizes, and it goes online in one click after you see the cost and approve.

An example of what you type

Make an Arabic landing page for an at-home car wash service with a simple booking form, and make sure it works well on phones.

AvailableOne-click publishing

A prototype people can try

The agent team splits your description between a developer, a reviewer and a coordinator to produce a prototype early customers can test. Every change stays logged in front of you.

Build a web prototype that manages gym memberships: member sign-up, renewals, and a reminder one week before a membership expires.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help entrepreneurs?

OPTES takes on the work that eats your time early on: market research, plans and decks, and building a landing page or prototype. You set the direction and review the results; it works within permissions you choose.

Do I need coding experience to build a site or prototype?

No. You describe what you want in plain words, and OPTES writes the code, tests it and shows it in a live preview. You request changes the same way, and nothing goes online until you see the cost and approve.

Is my business information protected?

OPTES hides secrets such as passwords and keys from the agent, and everything it does stays logged in front of you. Still, decide for yourself what sensitive data to share, as you would with any new tool or new hire.

How do I keep costs under control?

You set a spending cap, and any paid work stops when it is reached. Publishing only happens after you see its cost and approve, so you know what you will pay before you pay it.
