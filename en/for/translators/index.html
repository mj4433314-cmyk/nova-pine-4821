Translators

An AI assistant for translators: it drafts, you bring the craft

OPTES gives you a first translation draft that follows the glossary you provide, reads scanned and handwritten documents, transcribes recordings before you translate them, and checks terminology and figures for consistency between source and target. The craft stays with you: tone, precise meaning and your responsibility to the client.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help translators?

It takes on the repetitive parts: first drafts with your glossary, reading scanned documents, transcribing recordings, researching terms and checking consistency. Precise meaning, style and responsibility to the client stay with the translator.

Is it suitable for certified or legal translation?

It can prepare a draft, but certified translation needs a qualified translator who reviews every word, takes responsibility for it and signs it according to the official body's requirements. Never deliver a machine draft without a full review.

What about my clients' confidential documents?

Upload only what the task needs, honour your confidentiality agreements with clients, and remove unnecessary personal data before uploading where you can. What gets uploaded is always your decision.

Does it handle Arabic dialects?

Yes. It understands text and recordings in Modern Standard Arabic and in dialects, and writes in MSA or a specific dialect you ask for. Always review local idioms and proverbs, which need a translator's ear the most.
