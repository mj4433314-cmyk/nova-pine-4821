Photographers

An AI assistant for photographers that gives you back time to shoot

OPTES takes on what keeps a photographer away from the camera. It goes through a shoot and flags closed eyes, missed focus and near-duplicates, writes captions and alt text in two languages, drafts shot lists and event timelines, and organises files with a script. It also builds and publishes your portfolio site after you approve; your edit and style stay yours.

What OPTES does for you

First-pass cull of a shoot

Send the set of frames. OPTES flags closed eyes, missed focus and near-duplicates and suggests a shortlist; the final selection is yours.

An example of what you type

Here are 120 frames from the engagement shoot. Flag closed eyes and soft shots, and suggest the best 30 for the couple.

Captions and alt text

From the images and your notes it writes captions, descriptions and alt text in Arabic and English for your portfolio, social posts or a client gallery.

An example of what you type

Write a short caption and alt text for each photo in the Mutanabbi Street series, in Arabic and English, in a calm, understated tone.

Shot lists and event timelines

From a client's message it drafts the booking confirmation, the required shot list and the event-day timeline, in the tone you use with clients.

Organise your files

OPTES writes a script that renames files by date and client and sorts RAW and JPEG into folders, running it on a copy first so you can check.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help photographers?

With the work around the shoot: a first-pass cull, bilingual captions and alt text, shot lists and timelines, file organising, reference images and a portfolio site. Your eye and your style stay yours.

Does it edit or retouch my photos?

No. Editing and retouching stay with you and the software you already use. OPTES reads your photos to help you shortlist, describe and organise, and generates reference images for planning only.

Does it choose the final images for me?

No. It suggests a shortlist and flags obvious technical problems, but the final selection is your decision, because it is part of your style and of what you want the client to see.

Can it write to my clients in Arabic?

Yes. It writes booking confirmations, messages and captions in Modern Standard Arabic, the dialect you use with clients, or English, and keeps the tone you usually use with them.
