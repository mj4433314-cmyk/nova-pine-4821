Sales teams

AI assistant for sales teams: before the meeting and after it

Before a meeting, OPTES researches the company and its stated needs with sources. Afterwards it transcribes the recording, pulls out objections and next steps, and writes the follow-up and proposal in the client's language or dialect. It reads photographed business cards and documents and, with your approval, updates the client sheet on your computer. You build the relationship; it handles the paperwork.

What OPTES does for you

Meeting prep

Gathers what is public about the company, its business, recent news and branches, with sources, and suggests discovery questions. You verify the facts before mentioning them to the client.

An example of what you type

I'm meeting a pharmaceutical distributor in Erbil tomorrow. Summarise what's published about them and suggest five opening questions

AvailableResearch and summaries with sources

Recorded meeting summary

Upload the recording, once attendees have agreed to it, and it transcribes it and pulls out needs, objections and agreed next steps. You review the summary before sharing it.

An example of what you type

Transcribe the meeting recording, then give me the client's needs, their objections, and who follows up on what and when

AvailableSpeech to text

Follow-up messages

Writes the follow-up from the meeting summary in a tone that suits the client, in Arabic, English or their dialect. You review it and send it yourself.

An example of what you type

Write a follow-up that summarises what we agreed and proposes a demo next week, formal but warm

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Draft the proposal

Builds a written proposal from the client's needs and the prices you provide, with an executive summary and a line-item table. You check prices and terms before sending.

An example of what you type

Write a proposal to supply 40 point-of-sale terminals to a pharmacy chain at the attached prices, with a one-page executive summary

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Business cards and scanned documents

Photograph the business cards you collected at a trade fair and it reads them in Arabic and English into a table ready to import. You check names and numbers.

An example of what you type

These are photos of 25 business cards from the Baghdad International Fair. Put them in a CSV: name, company, title, phone, email

AvailableReading images and documents

Update the client sheet on your computer

With your approval, it controls the computer to move new client details into your sheet or system. You watch it work and can stop it at any moment.

An example of what you type

Move the ten clients from this CSV into the open client sheet, and stop to ask me about any unclear field

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help sales teams?

It handles the work around the sale: researching accounts before meetings, transcribing and summarising meetings, writing follow-ups and proposals, reading business cards and updating your sheets with your approval. Relationships and negotiation stay with you.

Will it send messages to clients on its own?

It sends nothing without your approval. It drafts and you review, and when it controls your computer you choose what needs your explicit OK, watch what it does and can stop it at any moment.

Can I record and summarise client meetings?

Yes, provided attendees know about the recording and agree to it, in line with the law and your company's policy. OPTES then transcribes it and extracts needs and next steps for you to review.

What about client data and pricing?

Upload only what the task needs and follow your company's policy on sharing client data. When it works on your computer, secrets such as passwords are hidden from the agent, and a log of what it did stays in front of you.
