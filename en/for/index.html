FAQ

Questions and answers

Will OPTES fit my profession if it isn’t listed?

Most likely: writing, research, coding, images, video and computer control serve most desk work. If you’d like a page for your profession, email support@optes3.com.

Does OPTES replace a doctor, a lawyer or an accountant?

No. It is an assistant that drafts, summarises and organises; it does not diagnose or give legal or tax rulings. The decision stays with the qualified professional, and everything it writes is reviewed before use.

Do I need technical skills to use it?

No. You tell it what you want in plain language, in Arabic, your dialect or English, and it plans, does the work and shows you the result. Anything that could touch your files, computer or spending goes through you first.
