HR teams

An AI assistant for HR teams: it handles the paperwork, you decide

HR teams are buried in job descriptions, CVs, attendance files and policies. OPTES writes fair job descriptions, prepares structured interview questions, organises CVs against criteria you set, and calculates attendance reports in code. It arranges information and judges no one; hiring and evaluation decisions always stay with a person on your team.

What OPTES does for you

Job descriptions

It writes a clear job description with the duties and qualifications the role really needs, in inclusive language without job-irrelevant conditions such as age or gender. You review it before posting.

An example of what you type

Write a job description for a junior accountant at a pharmaceutical distributor, focused on the skills the job really needs and with no age or gender requirements.

AvailableWriting, editing and translation

Organising CVs

It reads CVs and puts them into one consistent table based on criteria you set in advance, making comparison easier. Deciding who to interview stays with you.

An example of what you type

Put these thirty CVs into a table: years of experience, skills named in the job ad, and languages, without ranking or judging any candidate.

Structured interview questions

It prepares questions tied to the role's requirements, each with a clear scoring guide, so every candidate gets the same questions and the same yardstick.

Attendance and leave reports

FAQ

Questions and answers

How does OPTES help HR teams?

OPTES handles writing and organising: job descriptions, structured interview questions, CV tables, the employee handbook and attendance reports. Hiring and evaluation decisions stay with your team.

Does OPTES pick or reject candidates?

No. It organises information against job-related criteria that you define, and it makes no decision to accept or decline anyone. A person on your team reviews every candidate's file and decides.

How do we keep hiring fair?

Set your criteria before reading any CVs and tie them to the job's requirements, and ask OPTES to ignore age, gender, photos and marital status. Review the results yourselves; the tool helps but does not replace your judgement.

What about employees' personal data?

Share only what the task needs, hide ID numbers and salaries when they are not required, and follow your company's policies. OPTES hides secrets such as passwords from the agent, and what it did stays logged in front of you.
