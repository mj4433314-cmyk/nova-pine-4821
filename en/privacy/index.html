Privacy Policy

This page is written in plain language: what we store about you, what leaves your device, who we share it with, and what you control.

Who we are

OPTES is an independent project. OPTES is operated by Mohammed Riyadh, who is responsible for your data under this policy. For any question about your privacy or any request about your data, write to support@optes3.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google we receive your e-mail address and your name as shown on your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • E-mail from us. We sometimes send OPTES news and offers by e-mail; one click on the unsubscribe link in any of them stops them, while transactional e-mails (sign-in codes, receipts) continue.
  • Your devices. Each device gets its own key. We store a hash of your device key (not the key itself) to link the device to your account, and a device can be switched off from the server.
  • Device information. General technical facts about the device the app runs on (operating system, processor, memory, graphics card, screen size, language, time zone and app version), together with the network (IP) address the server sees. Details are in the «Device information» section below.
  • Usage. We record what is needed to apply your plan, limits and credit: when a request was made, its type, size, outcome and cost.
  • Payments. In Iraqi dinars you pay on the Wayl payment page; we never receive or store your card number. If you pay in cryptocurrency, the payment is processed by OxaPay: we send it the amount, the plan name and an order reference (not your name or e-mail), and it tells us the payment status, the coin, the network, the amount received and the transaction hash. Blockchain transactions are public by nature, and we never receive your wallet's private keys or recovery phrase. For both methods we keep a record of the transaction: amount, status and reference.
  • What you write to OPTES. The text of your requests and the files you give it are sent to our server and on to the model provider that produces the answer; the answer cannot be produced otherwise.
  • Conversation log for accuracy. We keep, encrypted and for 30 days at most, the last message you wrote in each request and the reply to it, with secrets removed automatically, to improve the accuracy of answers and fix errors. Details are in the «Conversation log for accuracy» section below.
  • Error reports. If you send a report from the app, we store it encrypted.
  • Reports of AI replies. If you tap «Report this reply», we receive the report category, your note, and the text of the reply if «Attach reply text» is on, together with the conversation's identifier, the name of the OPTES model that answered, your account number and the time. We use reports only for safety review and to improve answers, and delete them automatically within 90 days.
  • Server logs. Operation and security logs on the server (time and status of requests, network addresses, and errors) so we can find faults and abuse. They are deleted automatically within 30 days. A separate audit trail of account events (sign-ins, linked devices, plan and payment changes) holds no message text and is kept with your account.

Voice. When you use dictation, voice notes or spoken replies, the recording or text reaches our server and an external speech service so it can be converted to text or voice.

Camera and photos. Used only when you choose to send a photo or take one in the app.

Notifications. We keep your device's notification token to deliver alerts; you can turn them off in the phone settings.

The desktop app

Your conversations, files and projects are kept on your own device in a local database. Sign-in tokens are sealed with Windows encryption (DPAPI, through Electron safeStorage). Today conversations are not synced to our servers; what reaches us is what you send to be answered, as described above. We keep an encrypted copy of it, with secrets removed, for 30 days at most to improve accuracy (see «Conversation log for accuracy»). If we ever add optional sync, it will work only with your consent and we will update this page before turning it on.

On-device indexing (desktop app)

So that OPTES understands your work from the first conversation, the OPTES CODE desktop app for Windows learns your files and projects on your computer. This section applies from the OPTES CODE version that includes device indexing. The phone app and the web edition do not index your device.

When it starts. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, OPTES CODE asks you first, and indexing starts only if you agree. Everywhere else it is on by default after you sign in, and a clear notice tells you about it the first time. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & security → Device indexing.

What it reads.

  • Projects: folders that hold software or game projects (for example git, package.json, Unreal or Unity), their languages, frameworks and run commands, the branch and last commit, and a short excerpt of the README file.
  • Documents: names, types, sizes and dates of files, and the text inside common documents (txt, md, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF), within a size limit for each file and for the total text.
  • Photos, video and audio: name, type and date only. The content of images is not analysed.
  • Installed programs: name, version, publisher and installation date.
  • Recently opened files: names and dates from the Windows list of recent items, after removing everything listed below.

What it never reads. This list is fixed in the software, and no setting changes it: passwords and credential stores; browser data (cookies, history, saved passwords); password managers; mail and messages (Outlook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord, Thunderbird); .env and .ssh files, keys and certificates (such as pem, key, p12 and pfx); crypto wallets; OPTES’s own folders; system folders and programs; and the folders of other Windows accounts. OneDrive files kept only in the cloud are never downloaded; only their names are seen.

Where it is kept. The index is kept only on your computer, in the OPTES data folder, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key protected by your Windows account (DPAPI), so another account or another computer cannot open it. Secrets (API keys, tokens, written passwords, card numbers) are removed from any text before it is saved. A file that carries hidden instructions aimed at the AI is kept as data only, and its text is never given to the model.

What leaves your computer. Neither the index nor any original file is uploaded to our servers. When you chat with OPTES, the model can query the local index (search your files, look at your projects). Only the short answer, such as a project name, a file path or a short excerpt with secrets removed, becomes part of that conversation and is handled like any other message in it, as described in “Who we share data with”. Index data is never used to train any model.

Your choices.

  • Turn it off at any time: indexing stops at once.
  • Exclude a folder: it is removed from the index at once and never read again.
  • “Forget everything”: deletes the index and its key from your computer and turns indexing off. Your files themselves are not touched.

Shared and work computers. The index is separate for each Windows account and never reaches the folders of another account. On a work or shared computer, we recommend excluding the folders that are not yours, or turning the feature off. Questions: support@optes3.com.

Device information

The OPTES CODE app sends general technical facts about the device. They do not identify you, and the app sends them even before you sign in. We do this to know what kind of device is connecting and to detect abuse, such as registering large numbers of fake devices to get free usage unfairly, and to protect the service and its users.

  • What we collect. The operating system's name, version and architecture (for example Windows 11 Pro 24H2 and x64), the processor model and its number of cores, the memory size in gigabytes (rounded), the name of the main graphics card, the main screen's resolution and scale, the device's language and time zone, and the app version and update channel.
  • A random install ID. The app creates a random number once and keeps it in its own files. It is not a number from your hardware, it cannot be read from your device, and it changes if the app's data is deleted.
  • The network (IP) address. We store the address our server sees when you connect in the normal way, and the last 20 addresses with their times. If you connect through a VPN, a proxy or a rented server, the address we see is theirs, and we do not try to get around that in any way.

What we never collect. Your network MAC address, serial numbers of disks, the motherboard or any hardware part, the Windows machine ID, the computer name, the operating-system user name, file names or paths, installed apps, precise location, the clipboard, or contacts.

When it is sent. When the app registers its device for the first time, and then about once a day while the app is running.

How long we keep it and who sees it. We keep it while the device key is in use, and delete it when you ask us to. We do not sell it, we do not share it with advertisers and we do not use it for advertising. Only the OPTES team responsible for security and support can see it, and it is not shown in any user-facing screen. To ask for a copy or for deletion, write to support@optes3.com.

Conversation log for accuracy

To improve the accuracy of answers and fix errors, our server keeps a copy of each step of your conversations with OPTES, in the apps and on the web: the last message you wrote in the request, the final reply, the names of the tools the reply used, and the request's details: the time, your account number, the device, the plan, the model, and the request's outcome, duration and size.

  • Secrets are removed automatically. Before saving, the server removes anything that looks like a secret and puts «[removed]» in its place: service keys and access tokens, private keys, passwords, payment card numbers and bank account numbers (IBAN). It also masks the middle of e-mail addresses and phone numbers. This is automatic and can miss something, so it is best not to write secrets in a conversation.
  • Encrypted, for a limited time. We keep it encrypted (AES-256) and delete it automatically after 30 days at most.
  • What we do not keep here. The full conversation text that is resent with every request, the inputs and results of tools, and pictures.
  • Who reads it. Only the operator of OPTES, only to review accuracy and fix errors; it is not shown in any screen. We do not sell it, we do not share it with advertisers and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Deletion on request. To have your conversation log deleted before it expires, write to support@optes3.com from the e-mail linked to your account, and we delete all of it.

The web edition

Each account has an isolated workspace where your web files and conversations are kept, running on cloud infrastructure that OPTES rents. Your account, plan, credit and invoices are shared between the apps and the web. Conversations and files of the desktop app stay on your device and are not shared with the web today.

The Android app (Google Play)

The OPTES app on Google Play is for adults (18 and over) only.

Conversations and files. Your conversations in the app are stored on our servers so you can return to them, and we keep them until you delete them or delete your account (a very long conversation keeps its latest 600 messages, and an account its latest 1,000 conversations). When you attach a file, our server reads its text for the model: we do not keep the uploaded file itself (it is deleted once read, and within two days at the latest), and the text read from it stays with that conversation for up to 30 days so you can ask about it again. Files OPTES makes for you in a conversation are kept for up to 30 days. Pictures you send go to the model to be answered and are not stored as files.

Problem log. The app's problem log stays on your phone and reaches us only if you send it yourself by e-mail from «Report a problem».

Notifications. We use Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications; it receives only your device's notification token and the notification content.

Default assistant. If you make OPTES your default assistant, on-screen text is sent only when you tap to ask about it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and we show no advertising. We share only what is needed to run the service, with:

  • Third-party AI model providers: they receive the text of the request in order to answer it. The OPTES engine does not name them inside the conversation.
  • Google for sign-in and for the Google apps you choose to connect, and Wayl or OxaPay to complete payment.
  • Cloudflare, which serves this website and counts visits to it without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics).
  • The hosting and cloud providers that run our server and the web workspaces.
  • Public authorities, if the law requires it.

Google user data (Gmail and Google Drive)

You can connect your own Google account to OPTES from Apps → Connect. Before anything happens OPTES shows what it will be able to do, and then Google shows its own consent screen, where you decide. OPTES asks Google for:

  • Your Google e-mail address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.
  • Send e-mail on your behalf (Gmail · gmail.send): when you ask, OPTES writes an e-mail as an editable card in your conversation. It is sent from your Gmail only when you press «Send» yourself, and you have 10 seconds to undo. This permission does not let OPTES read, search or delete the e-mails in your mailbox.
  • Only the files you choose (Google Drive · drive.file): OPTES can open only the files you pick in Google's own file picker, and the files OPTES creates for you (for example a new sheet or document you ask for). It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

Features that read your mailbox (search, summaries, replies, archive, important-mail alerts) are offered today only to a few OPTES test accounts; if we offer them to you, Google will ask for your consent again and this page will say so first.

How we use it. Only to give you the feature you asked for in your conversation: writing and sending the e-mail you approved, or reading, summarising or editing the file you picked. To produce the answer, the text of that e-mail or file is sent to our server and on to the AI model provider that writes the reply, and for nothing else.

What we store and for how long. The connection tokens are encrypted on our server, separately for each account, and deleted when you disconnect (we also ask Google to revoke them). An e-mail card is kept encrypted while it is open (an unsent card expires after 30 minutes) and deleted about one day after it is sent, cancelled or expired. To stop mass mailing we keep only one-way hashes of recipient addresses, for two days. We do not keep copies of your Drive files; text from a file you asked about can appear in that conversation, which you can delete.

Limited Use. OPTES's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We send the AI model only the text needed to carry out your request, and we do not use your Google data to train models or for advertising. We do not sell it. People at OPTES do not read it, unless you ask us to (for example for support), it is needed to investigate security or abuse, or the law requires it.

Your control. Disconnect at any time from Apps → Gmail / Google Drive → Disconnect, or remove OPTES at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete everything we hold, write to support@optes3.com from the e-mail of your account.

How long we keep it

We keep account and usage data while your account exists, and some of it longer where needed to prove a payment or for security or legal reasons. We do not keep anything longer than we need. The periods today:

  • Conversations in the Android app and the web chat: until you delete them or your account.
  • Text read from a file you attach: up to 30 days; the uploaded file itself: deleted within two days at the latest; files OPTES makes for you: up to 30 days.
  • Reports of AI replies: deleted automatically within 90 days.
  • Server logs: deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Conversation log for accuracy: deleted automatically within 30 days, or sooner if you ask.
  • Gmail e-mail cards and recipient hashes: as described in the Google section above.
  • Payment records and the account audit trail: as long as the law requires.
  • Backups: your data leaves them as they expire, within 3 months at most.

When you delete your account, the account deletion page says what is deleted and when.

Your rights: deletion, export, correction

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and its data by writing to support@optes3.com from the e-mail linked to the account, or on the account deletion page. We may ask you to prove the account is yours. What we must keep by law (such as payment records) we will tell you about.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Provider keys and secrets stay on the server and never reach the app. Secrets are hidden from the agent, and the app holds only a limited device key. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a vulnerability, write to us through the security page.

Children

The service is not directed at anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Models and features marked 18+ are only for people aged 18 or older and open only after an age confirmation (see the Terms of Use). The OPTES app on Google Play is for adults (18 and over) only. If you learn that a child has registered, write to us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page, and for a material change we tell you in the app or by e-mail.
