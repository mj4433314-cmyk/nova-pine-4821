OPTES Cloud

FAQ

Before you start

Is ordering open now?

Yes. Pick a plan and a term and press «Order now»: the OPTES Cloud console opens with that plan selected. You need an OPTES account, the same one you use in the app; if you are not signed in, the console signs you in and brings you back.

Where are the servers?

In Germany, inside the EU. About 70 ms to the Middle East. More regions will follow.

What is the difference between shared and dedicated?

Shared vCPUs share physical cores and suit most sites and apps. Dedicated vCPUs are yours alone with steady performance, for heavy production and large databases.

Why SSD on shared plans and NVMe on dedicated?

That is what the hardware behind each plan provides, so we state it as it is. Size and type are written on every plan.

Payment and renewal

How do I pay?

From your OPTES Cloud wallet, separate from your chat-plan balance. Top it up in US dollars with cryptocurrency. Pick a term from 1 to 24 months.

What happens when the term ends?

It renews at the same price for the same term, paid from your OPTES Cloud wallet. If it is not renewed, the server is cancelled 2 days before the term ends (day 28 of a 30-day month), with a final notice 20 hours before. Auto-renew can be turned off any time.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes, from the panel; you pay only the difference for the remaining days. Downsizing works if the disk fits.

How are GPUs billed?

Per second from a prepaid balance. A GPU starts when your balance covers an hour, and stops by itself at zero or after 30 idle minutes.

Security and use

How do I log in?

With SSH using the public key you add when ordering. Passwords are off. If you lose your key, the browser console works from the panel.

Can you access my server?

No. We do not have your private key or any password, and we do not enter without your consent.

Why is port 25 blocked?

So the network never sends spam and our addresses stay clean. Use a mail service on port 587, or ask to open 25 after a month and we will review it.

What happens if my server is isolated?

We cut outbound traffic only; SSH from your addresses and the console keep working. We tell you the reason at once and we review it as fast as we can. Nothing is deleted.

Secure or Partners GPUs?

Secure means certified data centres. Partners are independent hosts, cheaper with no availability guarantee; never put sensitive data on them.

What is not allowed?

Mining, spam, scanning or attacking others, and illegal content. Details are in the acceptable use policy.
