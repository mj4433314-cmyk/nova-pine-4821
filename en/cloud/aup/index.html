OPTES Cloud

Acceptable use

Draft: awaiting legal review.

1. Who this covers

Every OPTES Cloud server and everyone who uses one. Using the service means you accept this policy together with the Terms.

2. Not allowed

• Spam, phishing and sender spoofing. • Scanning networks or addresses, and brute-forcing other people's systems. • Denial-of-service attacks and taking part in botnets. • Cryptocurrency mining of any kind. • Distributing malware or hosting its command-and-control. • Illegal content, including any child sexual abuse material, pirated material and stolen data. • Open proxies and public VPNs used for evasion or abuse. • Forging source addresses or impersonating other networks. • Reselling servers to third parties without our written consent.

3. Resources

Traffic and CPU stay within the plan's limits under a fair-use policy. Legitimate heavy load is fine; abusing shared resources is treated like any other violation.

4. Mail

Outbound port 25 is blocked by default. We open it on request after 30 days of good standing and a review, with correct SPF, DKIM and DMARC.

5. How we detect

We watch each server's network and CPU metrics from outside it and take abuse reports at abuse@optes3.com. We do not read your data or your disks.

6. How we act

• First signal: a notice. • Over a limit (spam, connection bursts): outbound traffic throttled for a while. • Ongoing scanning or outbound attack: automatic isolation that cuts outbound traffic and keeps your SSH and console access, with an immediate notice. • We review reports and isolations as fast as we can. • Confirmed or repeated violations: suspension or account termination, after a snapshot kept as evidence for as long as the law requires.

7. Your right to appeal

Ask for a review from the panel or by e-mail. If your server was compromised without your knowledge, we help you clean it up and bring it back.

8. Identity

We ask for light verification (a confirmed e-mail and a payment method) before the first server, and more before raising limits or opening port 25.

9. Authorities

We respond only to valid legal requests and tell you unless the law forbids it.

10. Changes

We give 14 days' notice of any material change to this policy.
