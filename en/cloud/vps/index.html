Specs every plan shares

Included in every plan at no extra cost.

Operating systems
Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Ubuntu 22.04 LTS, Debian 13, Debian 12 · Later: Rocky Linux 9, Rocky Linux 10, AlmaLinux 9, AlmaLinux 10, Fedora 43, openSUSE Leap 15.6, Arch Linux
One-click apps
Docker, Coolify, HestiaCP, WordPress, OpenClaw, Uptime Kuma, OPTES Bots, OPTES CODE (server) · Later: n8n
Security stack
DDoS protection, edge and host firewall, key-only SSH, CrowdSec, automatic security updates, port 25 blocked, automatic abuse isolation, KVM isolation per server, and two-factor confirmation for sensitive actions.
Backups
Encrypted daily backups kept 7 days, as an optional add-on at $1.85 a month per server. Snapshots are included in every plan. Includes 50 GB of stored backups; beyond that $0.009 per GB a month.
Traffic
Inbound is free. Outbound within the plan's allowance under a fair-use policy; beyond it $1.43 per TB.
Location
Germany (EU) · About 70 ms to the Middle East
Addresses
IPv4 + IPv6
Access and control
SSH with your key, a browser console, power and rebuild, a firewall editor with preview, snapshots and backups, and usage graphs.
Support
By e-mail at support@optes3.com. We review reports as fast as we can.

Billing and renewal

  • Renewal is paid from your OPTES Cloud wallet. A server that is not renewed is cancelled 2 days before its term ends: on day 28 of a 30-day month, and 2 days before the last day of a 1- or 2-year term. A final notice reaches you 20 hours before.
  • Renew before then and the server keeps running without a break.
  • Upgrade from the panel and pay only the difference for the remaining days.
