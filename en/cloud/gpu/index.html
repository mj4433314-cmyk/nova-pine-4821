Your money is protected

  • Prepaid only: a GPU starts when your balance covers an hour.
  • We warn you 30 and 10 minutes before the balance runs out, and stop it at zero.
  • Idle for 30 minutes and we stop it, with a 5-minute warning.
  • Each session lasts at most 12 hours; extend it any time.

GPU prices are in US dollars, deducted per second from your OPTES Cloud wallet (separate from your chat-plan balance). Top it up in US dollars with cryptocurrency.
