What every GPU comes with

  • A full machine: the GPU and its memory, plus vCPU, RAM and disk shown in the console before you start.
  • Billed per second from a prepaid balance; a start needs balance for one hour.
  • Jupyter, SSH and ready templates (PyTorch, vLLM, ComfyUI).
  • Stops by itself after 30 idle minutes or when the balance runs out, with a warning first.
  • The console shows each GPU's availability before you choose.
