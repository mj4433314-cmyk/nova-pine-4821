Included with every server

On from the first boot, with no setup on your side.

  • DDoS protectionLarge attacks are filtered before they reach your server.
  • Edge and host firewallInbound is blocked except what you allow, and the same rules apply outside the server.
  • Key-only SSHPasswords and root login are off, and SSH is limited to your address by default.
  • Intrusion preventionCrowdSec with a community blocklist bans brute-force attempts and known attackers.
  • Automatic security updatesInstalled daily; you choose the reboot window.
  • Port 25 block and abuse isolationDirect mail is blocked, and a hijacked server is isolated before it harms others while you keep access.

Location and payment

Where the servers are

In Germany, inside the EU, in certified data centres. About 70 ms to the Middle East. More regions later.
