User guide

The OPTES guide

Every step with a picture of the app itself, from the download to your first project. The numbers on the pictures match the text under them.

1Download and install

OPTES CODE for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) is downloaded from the official site only: optes3.com. Do not download it from any other link.

Check the fingerprint (optional, recommended): open PowerShell in your Downloads folder, run the command below and compare the result with the SHA-256 line on the site. If a single character differs, do not run the file; download it again.

Get-FileHash .\OPTES-Code-*-win-x64.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Windows SmartScreen: because the app is new, Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” on the first run. This is a general notice for recent apps, not a sign that the file is harmful.

  1. Click “More info”.
  2. Check that the file name starts with OPTES-Code and that you downloaded it from optes3.com.
  3. Click “Run anyway”.

The installer: OPTES CODE installs for your Windows account only; no administrator rights are needed.

  1. Choose the install folder, or keep the suggested one, then click Install.
  2. Wait for the progress bar to finish (usually a minute or less).
  3. Keep “Run OPTES CODE” ticked and click Finish. A shortcut is added to the desktop and the Start menu.

2First launch and sign-in

The app opens straight on the workspace in guest mode, so you can try it right away. Signing in makes your plan, usage and purchases follow your account on any device; your chats and files stay on your computer.

Linking the device

After you sign in, this device is linked to your account automatically, so your plan and usage show in the app. If a subscription asks you to link it, use “Link your device” in Settings → “Account & plan”. You can sign out from the same page at any time.

3A tour of the workspace

Everything is on one screen: the sidebar on the right, the composer in the middle and the chat tools under it.

4Choosing a model

The model is picked from the mode chip under the composer. The same menu sets when OPTES asks for your approval, and the effort level.

What does the lock mean?

A locked model exists and works, but it belongs to a higher plan. Clicking it costs nothing; it only shows the plan that opens it. The list in the picture comes from a demo; the models you get depend on your plan.

5Plans, subscribing and paying

Monthly prices in Iraqi dinars, fees included: Lite 6,000, Pro 19,000, Pro 2x 30,000, Pro+ 50,000, Max 144,000, Max Plus 288,000, Max Ultra 569,000 IQD. You pay on Wayl's secure page, or in cryptocurrency from outside Iraq; the app never asks for your card details.

You can also subscribe on the website: the plans page shows the same plans, and its “Subscribe” button takes you to “My account” on accounts.optes3.com.

Paying through Wayl:

  1. Click “Subscribe” on the plan you want (in the app or on the site).
  2. Sign in with Google if asked.
  3. On Wayl's page choose a payment method: bank card, SuperKi, Zain Cash or FIB, and complete the payment.
  4. Once confirmed, go back to the app: the new plan shows in “Account & plan” within moments (click “Refresh” if it is late).

6Adding your accounts and GitHub

If you already subscribe to another model service, sign in with it and its models show in the model list. Where: Settings → “Advanced” → “Advanced connection” → “Your own accounts”.

This is GitHub: to work on your GitHub repositories, pull requests and issues, switch on the GitHub skill in “Add-ons”.

Connecting your GitHub account

The skill uses GitHub's official command-line tool (gh). Sign in once with gh auth login, or ask OPTES “sign me in to GitHub” and it will guide you. Model accounts are added from “Your own accounts”.

7Your own API keys

Add an API key from any provider. The key stays on your computer and is hidden once saved; its models show in the list under the provider's name and do not count against your OPTES plan. Where: Settings → “Your own models”.

  1. Click “Get a key” next to the provider you want and sign in to your account there.
  2. Create a key and copy it.
  3. Back in the app, paste it into the key field.
  4. Open the model list: its models are there.

8Working on a project

OPTES works inside your project folder: it reads and edits files and runs commands, asking for approval as your mode says.

You can also just ask, for example: “Find the flower-shop project and understand it”. For a new project use “New project” in the sidebar.

9Updates

When a new version is out, a notice shows at the top of the screen. Nothing installs without your click, and updates come from optes3.com only.

10Usage and limits

Each plan has usage limits that renew by themselves. OPTES shows percentages and renewal times only, never amounts of money.

“Context window”, in the same popover, shows how full the current chat is; it appears after the first message.

11Troubleshooting and support

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”, after checking the file came from optes3.com and its fingerprint matches (section 1).

“The model is not available right now”.

The service is busy or being updated; you do not need to do anything. Try again shortly, or pick another model.

“You reached your session limit”.

The session limit renews by itself after the time in the message. Need more now? “Top up” or upgrade (section 5).

Where are the minimise, maximise and close buttons?

On Arabic Windows they sit at the top left of the window and no longer cover the app's buttons (since 1.2.6).

Sign-in is stuck on “Waiting for the browser”.

Finish in the browser that opened. If you closed it, click “Open the page again”, or “Cancel” and try again.

The update failed.

The reason shows under “Updates”, e.g. a dropped connection or a fingerprint mismatch (the file is deleted, never installed). Click “Try again”, or download the new version from the site.

Contact us

Email support@optes3.com with your version (from “About OPTES”) and what you saw. Never send passwords or keys. To report a security issue use the security page.
